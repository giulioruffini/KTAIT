% ── ABSTRACT ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
\begin{abstract}
\noindent We are building \lean{KTAIT}, a Lean~4 + Mathlib formalization whose purpose is
narrow and load-bearing: to \emph{machine-check that the Kolmogorov-Theory (KT) corollaries
follow logically from an explicit algorithmic-information-theory (AIT) interface}, and that
the KT ontology is typed so that structural errors (e.g.\ comparing a whole pattern to its own
part) cannot silently compile. We do \emph{not} re-prove classical AIT: the standard AIT and
probability results (the invariance theorem, the coding theorem, symmetry of information,
Kraft's inequality, Bayes' rule, the Solomonoff--Levin universal semimeasure, the recursion
theorem, Rice's and Chaitin's theorems, and the Vereshchagin--Vitányi structure-function
results) are taken as a clearly delimited \emph{axiom layer}. Every \emph{KT} corollary is then
\emph{proved} from that layer with no \lean{sorry}, and a toy model witnesses that the axiom
layer is jointly satisfiable (so the corollaries are not vacuously true). This working paper
states the methodology, fixes the axiom layer, inventories every theorem/lemma across the KT
corpus with its current formalization status, and sets a prioritized roadmap.
\end{abstract}

\vspace{0.5em}
\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} Kolmogorov Theory, algorithmic information theory, Lean~4, Mathlib,
formal verification, algorithmic regulator theorem, persistence, self-model.

\noindent\textbf{Classification:} MSC 68Q30, 03B35, 68V15 \quad/\quad ACM F.4.1

\section{Introduction and methodology}
\label{sec:intro}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

KT has matured to the point where the dominant risk is no longer in the classical results it
invokes, but in whether the \emph{new} KT corollaries are \emph{correctly typed} and
\emph{logically entailed} by those results. This project addresses exactly that risk with a
proof assistant.\cite{leanmathlib}

\subsection{The one rule}
\begin{thmbox}[The one rule]
Standard AIT (and probability) theorems are allowed to be \emph{axioms}. KT corollaries must be
\emph{proved} from those axioms with no \lean{sorry}.
\end{thmbox}
\noindent If a KT corollary needs a \lean{sorry}, that is a real gap in the paper. If a classical
AIT fact is an axiom, that is honest and expected: we are checking the \emph{dependency
structure}, not the AIT foundations.

\subsection{Why this is worth doing}
The bug class we most want to exclude is \emph{ontological}. For example, writing
$\Ik(A:\mathcal{S})$ where $\mathcal{S}\subset A$ is structurally vacuous (a whole versus its own
part); the correct object is \emph{temporal}, $\Ik(\mathcal{S}_t:\mathcal{S}_{t+\tau})$ or
$K(\mathcal{S}_{t+\tau}\mid \mathcal{S}_t,E,C)\ll K(\mathcal{S}_{t+\tau}\mid C)$. In \lean{KTAIT}
the KT roles (\emph{substrate, pattern, self-code, readout, regulator, time}) are \emph{distinct
types}; the only bridge to a common carrier is an explicit projection, so the bad version
\emph{fails to type-check}. The whole-vs-part error becomes a typed obligation, not a silent
mistake.

\subsection{Soundness: named hypotheses, not global axioms}
\label{sec:soundness}
A na\"ive encoding \lean{axiom F : forall (F : AITFrame), P F} asserting a law for \emph{every}
frame is \emph{inconsistent} here: one can hand-build a frame violating it (e.g.\ \lean{slack=0}
with mismatched $K$) and derive \lean{False}, which would make all corollaries vacuously true.
We therefore state each AIT fact as a \emph{named hypothesis} (a \lean{Prop} about a frame) and
let each corollary consume it. Consequently \lean{\#print axioms} on every KT corollary shows
only Lean's core axioms (\lean{propext}, \lean{Classical.choice}, \lean{Quot.sound}) --- strictly
stronger than ``rests on named AIT axioms.'' The toy model (\S\ref{sec:status}) then exhibits a
concrete frame satisfying the hypotheses, witnessing joint satisfiability.

\subsection{Scope}
The current effort is \textbf{Level~1} (a typed, axiomatic KT/AIT interface) plus
\textbf{Level~1.5} (the faithful \emph{probabilistic} ART, including its Bayesian posterior and
the coding-theorem bridge). Out of scope for now: a computability-backed $K$ from a concrete
universal machine (Level~2), and the differential-geometric (Lie-group / Noether) layer of the
world-models paper,\cite{ruffini2025worldmodels} which is a separate track.

\subsection{Citing the formalization}
\label{sec:citing}
Papers in the KT corpus cite this development with two macros. \lean{\textbackslash lean\{...\}}
marks incidental Lean text: a module filename, a definition, one of Lean's core axioms.
\ktait{\textbackslash ktait\{decl\}} is a machine-checked-claim citation: the prose at that
point asserts a result and names the KTAIT declaration that proves it, so which claims are
machine-checked is a lookup rather than an interpretation --- for a reader, and for any tool
indexing the corpus. Names inside \ktait{\textbackslash ktait} must be declarations; the guard
rejects filenames, paths, and core axioms there. Both macros are enforced: papers registered
in \lean{docs/citing-papers.txt} have every cited name resolved against the current
declarations by \lean{scripts/check\_sync.sh}, which the pre-push hook and CI run, so renaming
or restating a theorem cannot silently break a citing paper's claim. The canonical preamble
definition is \verb|\newcommand{\ktait}[1]{\texttt{\small #1}}|.

\section{The standard AIT / probability axiom layer}
\label{sec:axioms}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

These are the results we \emph{assume}. They are classical and not in doubt; collecting them here
fixes precisely what the KT proofs are allowed to lean on. (Per K's directive, starting from these
as axioms --- and similarly for Bayes --- is acceptable and intended.)

\begin{defbox}[Axiom layer A --- complexity]
\textbf{Invariance theorem.} $K$ is machine-independent up to an additive $O(1)$.\\
\textbf{Coding theorem (unconditional).} $c_1 2^{-K(x)}\le m(x)\le c_2 2^{-K(x)}$, i.e.
$-\log_2 m(x)=K(x)\pm O(1)$.\\
\textbf{Coding theorem (conditional).} $m(x\mid y)\asymp 2^{-K(x\mid y)}$.\\
\textbf{Kraft--McMillan.} $\sum_p 2^{-|p|}\le 1$, hence $\sum_x m(x)\le 1$.\\
\textbf{Symmetry of information.} $\Ik(x{:}y)=K(x)+K(y)-K(x,y)=K(x)-K(x\mid y^\ast)+O(\log)$;
chain rule $K(x,y)=K(x)+K(y\mid x^\ast)+O(1)$.\\
\textbf{Multiplicity compression.} $N_{\le L}(x)\ge 2^r \Rightarrow K(x)\le L-r+O(\log L)$.
\end{defbox}

\begin{defbox}[Axiom layer B --- probability / Bayes]
\textbf{Solomonoff--Levin universal semimeasure.} $m(x)=\sum_{U(p)=x}2^{-|p|}$; conditional
$m(R\mid W)\asymp 2^{-K(R\mid W)}$.\\
\textbf{Bayes' rule + deterministic likelihood.} prior $P(p)=2^{-|p|}$, likelihood
$P(x\mid p)=\mathbf{1}\{U(p)=x\}$, evidence $P(x)=m(x)$.\\
\textbf{Excess-length decay.} $\Pr\{|p|\ge K(x)+k \mid x\}\le 2C\,2^{-k}$.
\end{defbox}

\begin{defbox}[Axiom layer C --- computability / logic]
\textbf{Kleene's second recursion theorem} (quines); \textbf{Rice's theorem};
\textbf{Chaitin's incompleteness} ($T$ proves no $K(x)>n$ for $n>c_T$; $K$ uncomputable);
\textbf{Vereshchagin--Vitányi}: the Kolmogorov structure function $h_x(\alpha)$ is uncomputable
and minimal sufficient statistics are not locatable.

\smallskip
WP0007 consumes three of these in distinct forms, and \lean{AlgorithmicEmergence} keeps them
apart because they are not interchangeable: \lean{KUncomputable} ($K$ is not computable),
\lean{KThresholdUndecidable} (the predicate $K(x)<b$ is not \emph{decidable} --- it is
semidecidable, which is the whole content of the improvement barrier), and
\lean{KNotApproximable} (no computable $f$ satisfies $K\le f\le K+c$, which does \emph{not}
follow formally from the first as stated). \lean{chaitin\_certification\_ceiling} consumes the
Chaitin bound in the ``certified lower bounds are capped'' form.
\end{defbox}

\noindent\textbf{Proved KT corollaries (no \lean{sorry}; \lean{\#print axioms} = Lean core only):}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.4em,itemsep=1pt]
\item \lean{PP.token\_is\_agent}, \lean{PP.class\_is\_agent}, \lean{PP.collective\_is\_agent},
\lean{PP.kind\_is\_agent} --- the four cells of the WP0207 square (token $a_i$, constituent class
$\mathcal T$, collective $M$, collective kind $\mathcal L(M)$) are all agents. Possible only because
agenthood is a \emph{predicate} on the single carrier \lean{Pattern}, following WP0162's
``no agent over and above the pattern''.
\item \lean{PP.token\_not\_telehomeostatic}, \lean{PP.class\_is\_telehomeostatic} --- telehomeostasis
is fixed by the objective alone (WP0162 \S3), so a token whose objective proxies its class is a
genuine agent that is not telehomeostatic. \lean{PP.agentoptosis\_gap} states the conjunction: that
gap is the room agentoptosis occupies (WP0207). \lean{PP.token\_usually\_telehomeostatic} records the
default case: when the objective targets the token itself, it \emph{is} telehomeostatic, so the
divergence is the exception and not the rule.
\item \lean{PP.collective\_not\_telehomeostatic} --- the same divergence one grain up: a collective
instance whose objective proxies its lineage, with \lean{PP.kind\_is\_telehomeostatic} closing the
square. This is the snowflake-yeast sign anomaly as a typing fact rather than an exception.
\item \lean{PP.persists\_iff\_nmai} --- the module's persistence predicate unfolds definitionally
to WP0162's $\mathrm{NMAI}(S_t,S_{t+\tau})$. \lean{PP.Persists} is \lean{Persistence.Persistent} on
the pattern's trajectory, so persistence is inherited from \lean{Persistence.lean} rather than
restated: rational-valued, normalized, and lag-indexed.
\item \lean{PP.agent\_has\_temporal\_self\_model} --- because \lean{PP.IsAgent} is a positive
self-regulation gap $\Delta_{\mathrm{self}}$ (\lean{SelfModel.DeltaSelf}), Proposition~3 applies to
any agent verbatim: its future self-code is cheap given its present organization. Nothing is
re-proved; the same named hypotheses are consumed.
\item \lean{PP.externally\_regulated\_not\_agent}, \lean{PP.agent\_need\_not\_be\_telehomeostatic}
--- agenthood is computed from $\Delta_{\mathrm{self}}$, never asserted, so a pattern with no gap is
refused agenthood; and an agent whose objective targets another pattern is not telehomeostatic.
\item \lean{PP.room\_not\_agent}, \lean{PP.decoupled\_not\_agent} --- locus is \emph{derived} from a
directional ablation pair, never asserted, so the externally regulated thermostat room and the
decoupled pattern are refused agenthood by computation.
\item \lean{AITProb.probabilistic\_regulator\_theorem} --- ART Theorem~2,
$\Pr((W,R)\mid x,E)\le C\,2^{\,M(W:R)}2^{-\Delta}$.
\item \lean{AITProb.wrapper\_bound} --- ART Eq.~(6), now \emph{derived} from the coding theorem:
$\mathrm{post}(e,x)\le (1/c_1)2^{K(x)-K(e)}$.
\item \lean{AITProb.probabilistic\_regulator\_theorem\_sharp} --- ART Theorem~2 \emph{sharp form}
retaining the regulator cost: $\mathrm{post}\le C\,2^{M(W:R)}2^{-\Delta}2^{-K(R)}$ (no extra
hypothesis; the headline drops $2^{-K(R)}\le 1$ via \lean{probabilistic\_regulator\_theorem\_of\_sharp}).
\item \lean{AITProb.theorem1\_posterior\_tilt} --- ART Theorem~1, two-sided posterior tilt.
\item \lean{AITProb.theorem3\_onoff\_evidence} --- ART Theorem~3, ON/OFF evidence ratio
$\asymp 2^{\Delta}$ (log-free form).
\item \lean{CoarseGraining.theoremB} / \lean{corollaryB} --- WP0193 Theorem~B / Corollary~B:
regulatory coarse-graining is uncomputable, by reduction to Vereshchagin--Vitányi.
\item \lean{quine\_floor}, \lean{self\_prediction\_dichotomy}, \lean{chaitin\_blocks\_minimality}
--- WP0192 Principle~1 / WP0162 Proposition~4: the three independent obstructions to a complete,
exact, certifiable self-model (the middle one is an axiom-free diagonalization).
\item \lean{contrast\_posterior\_ranks\_by\_complexity} --- WP0162 Q3 (Eq.~26): the contrast-fiber
posterior ranks by joint simplicity, with no $2^{-\Delta}$ tilt.
\item \lean{transform\_preserves\_IK}, \lean{shift\_is\_not\_a\_null},
\lean{cheap\_transform\_not\_null} --- WP0202 Appendix~A. If $y=T_i(x)$ is computable from $x^\ast$
plus a short index, then $\Ik(x:T_i x)\ge K(T_i x)-K(\mathrm{code}\,i)-3\,\mathrm{slack}$: a
\emph{cheaply-computable transform preserves algorithmic mutual information}. The corollary names an
error that actually shipped into a draft --- a \textbf{circular shift is not a null} for whole-object
MAI, since $K(\sigma_s(x)\mid x^\ast)\le K(s)+O(1)=O(\log n)$, so the shifted copy retains nearly all
of its algorithmic information with the original. Using it as a surrogate subtracts the signal, and a
real screening effect was consequently withdrawn as ``indistinguishable from noise''.
\lean{cheap\_transform\_not\_null} states the contradiction directly: no $\varepsilon$ below
$K(\sigma_s x)-K(s)-3\,\mathrm{slack}$ can satisfy \lean{IsNullFor}. (A time-aligned mutual
information \emph{rate} is a different object, for which a shift \emph{is} a valid null; that object
is not an AIT quantity and is not formalized here. Keeping the two apart is the whole point.)
Satisfiability witness: \lean{ShiftFrame}, in which the bound is tight,
$\Ik(x:\sigma_s x)=K(x)$.
\item \lean{PushPull.theorem1\_necessity} --- WP0186 Theorem~1. Against a free two-sided
disturbance ($\underline{D}<0<\overline{D}$), holding a band $[x_-,x_+]$ requires pull authority at
the upper edge at least $\overline{D}$ and push authority at the lower edge at least
$-\underline{D}$. Its contrapositive, \lean{single\_signed\_cannot\_regulate}, is the paper's
headline necessity claim: a single-signed controller cannot hold the band. Theorem~2
(\lean{theorem2\_sufficiency}) shows the saturating edge rule attains those conditions, and
\lean{regulation\_iff} assembles the two halves into the equivalence the paper states in pieces;
\lean{inward\_iff} is the step that collapses the $\forall D$ condition to its two worst cases.
Formalizing them showed these are \emph{not} dynamical theorems: their proofs evaluate the field
only at $x_-$ and $x_+$, so their content is arithmetic on two boundary inequalities. The dynamics
enter only through Nagumo's theorem, which is \emph{assumed} (see below) and is never used to
derive the sign conditions.
\item \lean{PushPull.nonstrict\_fails\_attraction} and its repair
\lean{margin\_gives\_uniform\_decrease}
--- a defect in WP0186's Theorem~2, and the fix. The first is a \emph{counterexample}: a plant
meeting every hypothesis of the sufficiency theorem (band $[-1,1]$, disturbance range $[-1,1]$, unit
authorities, saturating rule) whose closed-loop field vanishes at $x=5$ under the admissible
realization $D\equiv\overline{D}$, so the constant trajectory $x(t)\equiv 5$ never enters the band.
Invariance survives, ultimate boundedness fails: the escape clause ``strict inequality whenever
$D<\overline{D}$'' is vacuous because $D\equiv\overline{D}$ is itself admissible. The repair supplies a strict
off-band margin $\varepsilon>0$, giving $\dot x\le-\varepsilon$ and hence entry in
time $\le(x_0-x_+)/\varepsilon$. The asymmetry is real: the necessary condition is tight and
non-strict, while sufficiency for attraction needs one $\varepsilon$ of slack.
\item \lean{PushPull.sign\_diverse\_card\_ge\_two}, \lean{PushPull.minimal\_is\_two} --- WP0186's
cardinality claim, with the distinction Lean forced into the open: the theorems bound \emph{signs},
not counts. A regulating flow set must contain at least one flow of each sign, whence
$|S|\ge2$; and two flows, one of each sign, achieve it, so the bound is tight. Two pushes do not
regulate however many there are.
\item \lean{PushPull.restoring\_not\_single\_signed}, \lean{PushPull.restoring\_decomposition} ---
WP0186 Corollary~1. For $\kappa>0$ the leaky-integrator field $-\kappa(x-x^\star)$ is strictly
positive below $x^\star$ and strictly negative above it, so no single sign-constrained flow realizes
it; and it decomposes explicitly into a non-negative push active below $x^\star$ and a non-negative
pull active above. The single-term restoring law is a push--pull motif in collapsed notation, not a
counterexample to one.
\item \lean{PushPull.lemma1\_gap\_identity}, with its existential restatement
\lean{lemma1\_gap\_is\_S\_existential} --- WP0186's complexity-gap lemma, against the opaque
\lean{AITFrame} carrier. From (A2)$+$(A3) (\lean{OffSplit}: $K(x_{\rm OFF})=K(D_{\rm pred})+K(\eta)$
within slack) and (A4) (\lean{OnResidual}: $K(x_{\rm ON})=K(\eta)$ within slack), the gap is
$\Delta=S\pm2\,\mathrm{slack}$ with $S:=K(D_{\rm pred})$: the residual $K(\eta)$, possibly
$\Theta(N)$, cancels. The regulator's dynamics play no role --- the assumptions, not the mechanism,
carry the lemma. \lean{no\_structure\_no\_gap} is the degenerate case: if the predictable component
is trivial ($S=0$) the gap vanishes up to slack, so a regulator that caps the amplitude of
unpredictable disturbance claims no model content. \textbf{A retraction is recorded with these
theorems.} An earlier draft chained the identity into ART to conclude that a sustained $\Delta$
\emph{forces} model content $M(W{:}R)\gtrsim\Delta$. That inference is not licensed and the paper no
longer makes it; the type-level tell is that $\Ik$ does not occur in the statement at all, and no
arithmetic on \lean{OffSplit}/\lean{OnResidual} will introduce it. WP0186's Proposition~1 is now a
selection claim, which belongs with \lean{RegulatorSelection.lean}.
\item \lean{ModelOrPay.simple\_regulator\_cannot\_model} --- WP0203 Proposition~1, from the single
AIT fact that module assumes, \lean{MAICapped} ($\Ik(x{:}y)\le K(y)+\mathrm{slack}$, a named
hypothesis about a frame). If $K(R)+\mathrm{slack}<\Delta$ then $\Ik(W{:}R)<\Delta$ for
\emph{every} world: a regulator simpler than the gap it opens cannot carry $\Delta$ bits of model.
Contrapositively (\lean{model\_implies\_big}), a pair satisfying the modelling disjunct
$\Delta\le\Ik(W{:}R)$ has $\Delta\le K(R)+\mathrm{slack}$ --- ``model it'' implies ``be big.'' The
clamp then needs no special pleading: by
\lean{clamp\_MAI\_bounded\_by\_own\_complexity}, $K(R)=O(1)$ alone caps its mutual algorithmic
information with every world.
\item \lean{ModelOrPay.perpair\_does\_not\_lift} and \lean{multiplicity\_cancels\_penalty}
--- WP0203 Proposition~2, the multiplicity objection, as \emph{witnesses} rather than assertions.
For every $k$ there is a family of $2^k$ terms each of weight at most $2^{-k}$ whose total is
exactly $1$; and $2^{k}\cdot2^{-k}=1$. A per-pair bound therefore places no useful bound on a sum
over a fiber of matching size --- the ART per-pair penalty is real and is exactly consumed by the
multiplicity. These are counterexamples to the lifting step; they establish no forward tail bound
and are not evidence for one.
\item \lean{ModelOrPay.invertible\_bound\_insufficient} --- a second \emph{insufficiency} witness. The
invertible-coupling inequality $\Ik(W{:}R)\ge K(R)-K(x_{\rm ON})-O(\log)$ bounds in
$K(R)-K(x_{\rm ON})$, not in $\Delta=K(x_{\rm OFF})-K(x_{\rm ON})$: the values $K(R)=1$,
$K(x_{\rm ON})=0$, $K(x_{\rm OFF})=100$, $\Ik=1$ satisfy it while leaving $\Ik$ far below the gap.
The two bounds coincide exactly when $K(x_{\rm OFF})\le K(R)$
(\lean{invertible\_reaches\_gap\_iff}), which is the hypothesis nothing supplies and a clamp
violates. What is machine-checked is that the invertible-coupling bound is \emph{insufficient};
nothing here establishes the positive claim it was meant to support.
\item \lean{OrbitLabel.genEnergy\_conserved} --- WP0162 App.~C: generalized energy as a conserved
orbit label (axiom-free).
\item \lean{boundary\_sufficient} --- WP0162 App.~E: the thin boundary as a compressed sufficient
statistic.
\item \lean{low\_complexity\_shrinkage} --- ART Theorem~A4; \lean{CoarseGraining.theoremA},
\lean{CoarseGraining.existence} --- WP0193 Theorem~A and Proposition~1.
\item \lean{Noether.trajLabel\_conserved}, \lean{Noether.conserved\_comp\_symm} --- the abstract
core of generalized Noether (Structured Dynamics paper): the trajectory label is conserved by the
flow, and symmetries commuting with the flow act on conserved quantities. (Continuous-time analogue
of the orbit-label theorem.)
\item \lean{Homogeneous.homogeneousSpace} --- a transitive symmetry action makes the state space a
homogeneous space, $X \simeq G/H_x$ (Theorem A4's algebraic core; the manifold/dimension layer
remains future work).
\item \lean{Decoder.achievable\_computable\_of\_inverse} / \lean{Decoder.no\_universal\_decoder}
--- WP0058 Proposition~1(i)--(ii): a \emph{total} computable write-back encoder that recognizes its
own domain would decide membership in the achievable set of the developmental map; hence none exists
(\lean{exists\_map\_without\_decoder}), and every realized write-back channel is a partial map whose
validity domain must be supplied or learned rather than recognized by the encoder itself.
Notable as the one KT corollary here that assumes \emph{no} AIT fact at all --- the engine
(\lean{achievable\_computable\_of\_inverse}) is unconditional, and only the non-vacuity of the
undecidability hypothesis enters as a named \lean{Prop}
(\lean{ExistsUndecidableAchievableSet}). Satisfiability witness: \lean{inverts\_id}.
\item \lean{Decoder.partial\_inverse\_exists} / \lean{Decoder.achievable\_rePred} --- the two
positive halves of the same proposition, which locate exactly what the impossibility costs.
Dovetailing search gives a \emph{partial} inverse that returns a genuine preimage wherever it is
defined, so inversion as such is available; what is lost is the ability to \emph{refuse}, since
the search diverges off the achievable set. Correspondingly the achievable set of a computable
developmental map is recursively enumerable, which machine-checks the informal half of WP0058's
proof (``enumerate programs, run $D$, collect outputs'') and leaves only undecidability assumed.
\item \lean{WriteBack.channel\_bound} --- WP0058's channel-bound lemma as a single named result
covering both links, $\Ik(a:H'\mid w)\le \Ik(a:z\mid w)\le K(z\mid w)+O(\log)$. The right link is
\lean{condIK\_le\_condRight}; the left is data processing, assumed as \lean{DataProcessingCond}.
Cite this for the whole chain and \lean{condIK\_le\_condRight} for the second link alone.
\item \lean{WriteBack.darwinian\_of\_dataProcessing} --- a Darwinian regime is exactly the general
data-processing hypothesis instantiated at the selection signal, so \lean{Darwinian} assumes
nothing beyond \lean{DataProcessingCond} plus the caller's Markov premise that $\sigma$ is the
only route from $a$ into $H'$.
\item \lean{WriteBack.bandwidth\_le\_cond}, the channel bound, and the regime corollaries
--- WP0058 Proposition~3. Writing $\lambda_B$ for the write-back bandwidth (defined below on the
write-back program, \emph{not} as $\Ik(a:H'\mid H)$), the master
bound $\lambda_B \le K(H'\mid H)+O(\log)$ holds but is weak in the Darwinian regime, where undirected
mutation makes $K(H'\mid H)$ large without transmitting anything acquired. The sharp statement is data
processing (\lean{condIK\_le\_condRight}): since $a$ reaches $H'$ only through a channel variable $z$,
$\lambda_B \le \Ik(a:z\mid H) \le K(z\mid H)+O(\log)$. Crucially, the write-back bandwidth is \emph{not} $\Ik(a:H'\mid H)$: that quantity is nonzero even in a
strictly Darwinian lineage, since acquired structure influences \emph{who reproduces}. Mutual information
cannot isolate direct write-back --- directness is causal, not correlational --- so WP0058 conditions on the
selection signal, $\lambda_B := \Ik(w : H'\mid H,\sigma)$ on the tagged write-back program $w=C(a)$, which attaches the quantity to the channel rather than inferring it from residual dependence, and blocks $a\to\sigma\to H'$ (\lean{writeBack}). No additive split $\lambda_{\rm sel}=\Ik(a:H'\mid H)-\lambda_B$ is defined: conditional mutual information is not additively decomposable. Then: in the Darwinian regime there is no write-back program, so $\lambda_B\le O(\log)$
(\lean{no\_program\_no\_write\_back}) --- the premise is the \emph{absence of the write-back program}, architectural and posited, since no information measure certifies a missing causal path --- while the \emph{total} information crossing is bounded by the
selection signal, $\Ik(a:H'\mid H)\le K(\sigma)+O(\log)$ (\lean{darwinian\_bandwidth\_le\_selection},
zeroth-order): that bound is the substantive half. In the Lamarckian regime
$\lambda_B \le K(w\mid H,\sigma)+O(\log)$ (\lean{lamarckian\_bandwidth\_le\_decoder\_image}, first-order) --- immediate, since $\lambda_B$ is \emph{defined} on $w$.
\lean{toyWB\_writeback\_zero\_but\_total\_positive} machine-checks that the two quantities genuinely differ:
a witness with $\lambda_B=0$ and total $=3$. Also \lean{decoder\_charged} ($K(C)\le K(H)+O(\log)$, Prop.~2's background-free form) and
\lean{trivial\_decoder\_transmits\_nothing} (Corollary~1: a trivial write-back program transmits
nothing however much was acquired). Witness \lean{ToyWB} satisfies both named AIT hypotheses \emph{and
attains} the Darwinian bound (\lean{toyWB\_selection\_bound\_tight}) --- the corollaries are tight,
not merely non-vacuous.
\item \lean{WriteBack.decoder\_charged\_rel} --- WP0058 Proposition~2 proper, the background-relative form.
\lean{decoder\_charged} charges the \emph{whole} write-back apparatus to the heritable program, but
part of it is supplied by a background $B$: the cell, the parent, the niche, a teacher, an external
training pipeline. Under conditional subadditivity relative to a background
(\lean{SubadditivityCondRel}, a named \lean{Prop}) an encoder reconstructible from $H$ and $B$
jointly (\lean{RecoverableFromRel}) costs the lineage only $K(C\mid B)\le K(H\mid B)+O(\log)$. The
hypothesis is strictly weaker than \lean{RecoverableFrom} (\lean{toyWB\_background\_strictly\_weaker})
and the bound is attained away from zero (\lean{toyWB\_decoder\_charged\_rel\_tight}); \lean{ToyWB}
satisfies it (\lean{toyWB\_subadditivityCondRel}). What is \emph{not} shown, here or anywhere in the
module: that write-back cannot produce novelty, or that the encoder's representational axes were
found by prior selection.
\item \lean{WriteBack.retention\_le\_program} --- the retention curve
$M(n)=\Ik(w:H_{g+n}\mid H_g)$ (\lean{retention}) is bounded at every lag by $K(w\mid H_g)+O(\log)$:
what survives never exceeds what was sent. A corollary of \lean{condIK\_le\_condRight}.
\item \lean{Retention.bangbang\_optimal} --- the optimal inherited-memory kernel is a
\emph{threshold rule}. With payoff $J=\sum_{n<N} q(n)\,v(n)$ linear in a box-constrained kernel
$q:\mathbb N\to[0,1]$ (\lean{J}, \lean{Admissible}) and $v(n)$ the net value of still acting on
$n$-generation-old information, every admissible kernel is dominated termwise by
$q^\ast(n)=\mathbb 1[v(n)>0]$ (\lean{qStar}, with \lean{qStar\_of\_pos},
\lean{qStar\_of\_nonpos}, \lean{qStar\_admissible}). If $v$ is antitone the retained set is an
initial segment (\lean{optimal\_kernel\_antitone\_is\_initial\_segment}), so the optimum is
``retain for $L$ generations, then drop'', and $L$ is the \emph{first crossing}:
$q^\ast(n)=1 \iff n<L$ (\lean{qStar\_eq\_one\_iff\_lt\_first\_crossing},
\lean{qStar\_eq\_zero\_of\_first\_crossing\_le}) for $L$ satisfying \lean{FirstCrossing}, which
exists whenever $v$ is somewhere nonpositive (\lean{exists\_firstCrossing}).
\item \lean{Retention.persistence\_is\_first\_crossing} --- the punchline. Instantiating
$v(n)=R(n)-\kappa$ (\lean{netValue}, antitone by \lean{netValue\_antitone}) with $R$ the predictive
information the environmental feature still carries at lag $n$ and $\kappa>0$ the per-generation cost
of carrying it plus the mismatch loss, the optimum retains exactly the lags where $\kappa<R(n)$.
Optimal persistence is therefore the first lag at which $R$ crosses the cost threshold --- WP0058's
$\tau_{\rho,\theta}$ with $\theta=\kappa$. The matching relation is the \emph{solution} of a linear
retention problem, not a modelling choice, and the threshold is set by the cost.
\item \lean{Retention.exponential\_crossing} --- for $R(n)=R_0e^{-n/\tau}$ with $R_0,\tau>0$ and
$\kappa>0$, $\kappa<R(n) \iff n < \tau\log(R_0/\kappa)$, so the optimal kernel is the indicator of
that interval (\lean{qStar\_exponential}) and the crossing lag is \emph{proportional} to the
environmental correlation time at fixed cost ratio
(\lean{exponential\_persistence\_proportional\_to\_tau}). Non-vacuity: \lean{vTest\_antitone},
\lean{vTest\_firstCrossing} and \lean{toyRetention\_qStar\_beats\_full} exhibit an antitone net value
whose optimum is a nontrivial initial segment and strictly beats full retention ($3>2$).
What is \emph{not} proved: that any real channel sits at this optimum, that the payoff is linear in
$q$, that costs are constant in $n$, or that decay is exponential. The empirical matching claim
remains Hypothesis~1.
\item \lean{persistence\_conservation} / \lean{conservation\_tradeoff} --- WP0162 Proposition~2:
the ledger $K(O_W)=\Ik(O_W{:}R)+K(O_W\mid R^\ast)$ (from symmetry of information), and at fixed
$K(O_W)$, maximizing $\Ik$ $=$ minimizing the conditional residual.
\item \lean{meta\_persistence} / \lean{meta\_persistence\_limit} --- WP0162 \S6 (meta-persistence):
a collective submodel of stable complexity $k>0$ with a bounded transient
$K(S^C_t\mid(S^C_{t+\tau})^\ast)\le L$ (delivered by closure $+$ a Foster--Lyapunov contraction of
the constituent flow) has collective persistence $\mathrm{Pers}_C\ge 1-(L+\mathrm{slack})/k$, hence
$\to 1$ as $k\to\infty$ --- with \emph{no} shared or compatible objective among the parts (the
hypotheses mention only $S^C,k,L,\mathrm{slack}$). Non-vacuity: \lean{toy\_meta\_persistence\_fires}.
\item \lean{regulator\_selection} --- WP0162 Proposition~1: $\argmin_R K(R\mid W)=\argmax_R[M(W{:}R)-K(R)]$.
\item \lean{regulator\_selection\_order} --- the order form of the same proposition: under the chain
rule, minimizing $K(R\mid W)$ is equivalent to maximizing $M(W{:}R)-K(R)$, as an order-equivalence
rather than an argmin identity.
\item \lean{AlgorithmicEmergence.no\_universal\_compressor} /
\lean{no\_universal\_compressor\_micro} --- WP0007 Theorem~2 (improvement barrier) in the form
that carries the intuition: no total computable compressor returns, for every string admitting a
description shorter than itself, such a description. Asks nothing about optimality. Consumes
\lean{CompressibleUndecidable}, the undecidability of $\{x:K(x)<|x|\}$, which is a \emph{different}
classical fact from \lean{KThresholdUndecidable} --- a diagonal slice of an undecidable set need
not be undecidable.
\item \lean{AlgorithmicEmergence.compressor\_of\_threshold} --- the hierarchy, recorded as a lemma
because it is easy to assert backwards. A threshold procedure specializes at $b=|x|$ to a
universal compressor, so ``no universal compressor'' \emph{implies} ``no threshold procedure''.
The raw-length statement is the stronger one and is \textbf{not} a corollary of the
arbitrary-$b$ theorem; the converse fails, since a procedure required to work only at $b=|x|$ is a
weaker object than one required to work at every $b$.
\item \lean{AlgorithmicEmergence.no\_compression\_improver} /
\lean{no\_compression\_improver\_micro} --- WP0007 Theorem~2 (improvement barrier): no computable
procedure returns, for every string (resp.\ micro-experiment) and threshold $b$, a description
shorter than $b$ whenever one exists. Strictly weaker a demand than optimality, and still
impossible: validity already gives $\mathrm{len}(A(x,b))<b \Rightarrow K(x)<b$, so the threshold
guarantee would make the undecidable predicate $K(x)<b$ decidable. The AIT input is
\lean{KThresholdUndecidable}. The companion observation --- that the predicate is
\emph{semidecidable}, so open-ended search finds a shorter description whenever one exists and
only fails to halt otherwise --- is deliberately not formalized: it would need an operational
semantics for programs, a halting-in-$t$-steps predicate and the dovetailing construction. The
theorem rules out a \emph{total} procedure that halts on every input while exploiting every
available compression; semidecidability is the complementary positive fact about partial search,
so nothing proved here rests on it.
\item \lean{AlgorithmicEmergence.identification\_barrier} --- WP0007 Theorem~3: no computable
procedure returns, for every finite micro-experiment, a shortest program for its macrodata.
``Reduction is not construction.'' The reduction runs along the \lean{Faithful} shift-and-read
embedding of WP0007 Theorem~1; the AIT input is the uncomputability of $K$, assumed as the named
hypothesis \lean{KUncomputable}. \lean{identification\_barrier\_conditional} is the same statement with
complexity taken conditional on the microlaw and observation map; it is a variant kept for the
development's own sake, and is \emph{not} what WP0007's conditional-form corollary says --- that
is \lean{conditional\_complexity\_uncomputable} above. WP0007's numbering has shifted three times
as results were inserted ahead of others, so entries here name results rather than number them.
\item \lean{AlgorithmicEmergence.no\_additive\_approximation} --- WP0007 Proposition~1: relaxing
``shortest'' to ``within a fixed additive constant $c$ of shortest'' does not restore
computability, for any $c$. The AIT input here is \lean{KNotApproximable}, which is \emph{not}
implied by \lean{KUncomputable} as stated --- it is the Berry argument run with slack, proved in
WP0007 Proposition~1 and assumed here.
\item \lean{AlgorithmicEmergence.counting\_bound} / \lean{few\_low\_complexity\_strings} /
\lean{most\_states\_incompressible} --- WP0007 Theorem~1 (existence barrier), proved outright
rather than assumed: distinct strings need distinct shortest programs, so fewer than $2^{m}$
strings have a shortest conditional program of length $<m$, and the complement is therefore more
than a $1-2^{-d}$ fraction of the state space. The bound holds for every \emph{fixed} conditioning
string shared across the ensemble and does not depend on it --- no choice of a common microlaw and
observation map improves the fraction.
\item \lean{AlgorithmicEmergence.conditional\_complexity\_uncomputable} --- WP0007's conditional-form corollary:
conditional complexity relative to $(D_n,C_n,n)$ is not computable. The conditioning
\emph{varies with the input}, so this does not follow from uncomputability at a fixed conditioning
string; the formalization carries the paper's diagonal argument, with the counting witness and the
constant-size search as its two named hypotheses.
\item \lean{AlgorithmicEmergence.chaitin\_certification\_ceiling} --- WP0007 Proposition~2:
certified complexity lower bounds are capped at a constant fixed by the theory and the reference
machine, so no macromodel longer than that constant is certifiably shortest for any record. Same
obstruction as \lean{chaitin\_blocks\_minimality}, stated for macromodel optimality rather than
self-models. Uncomputability is uniform over the family; unprovability is per-instance.
\item \lean{algorithmic\_emergence} --- \textbf{superseded.} This declaration stated WP0007's
former ``no algorithm finds the optimal coarse-graining'' result. WP0007~v0.3.0 withdrew that
theorem: the objective is degenerate under a constant observation map, the target moves as $C$
varies, and no reduction from the unrestricted structure function into the induced subclass was
ever given. The declaration is retained only because \lean{corollaryB} documents the regulatory
case, where WP0193 fixes the target and the reduction does go through. Cite
\lean{identification\_barrier} for WP0007.
\item \lean{AITProb.probabilistic\_regulator\_theorem\_conditional} --- ART sharp form in the
$2^{-K(R\mid W)}$ reading (posterior favors regulators simple given the world).
\item \lean{PrefixMachine.lemma1\_posterior\_bounds} --- the Bayes$\leftrightarrow$Kolmogorov bridge,
$\tfrac{1}{c_2}2^{K(x)-|p|}\le P(p\mid x)\le \tfrac{1}{c_1}2^{K(x)-|p|}$.
\item \lean{self\_regulation\_temporal\_model} --- Proposition~3,
$K(S_{t+\tau}\mid S_t,E,C)\le K(S_{t+\tau}\mid C)-\Delta_{\rm self}+2\,\mathrm{slack}$.
\item \lean{pers\_eq\_nmai}, \lean{persistent\_pos} --- persistence as temporal self-information.
\end{itemize}

\noindent\textbf{Assumed bridges, and what is deliberately not formalized.}
\lean{PushPull.lean} assumes one classical control-theory fact --- \emph{Nagumo's theorem}, that for
a locally Lipschitz field on a closed interval an inward-pointing boundary condition is equivalent
to forward invariance --- as the predicate \lean{NagumoBridge}. This is the dynamical counterpart of
not re-proving the coding theorem. It is an assumed bridge, not a KT result, and nothing in the
module derives the sign conditions from it. \lean{ModelOrPay.lean} assumes \lean{MAICapped} and
nothing else. The headline conjecture it was written to check --- that a faithful regulator
sustaining $\Delta_N$ either has
$\Ik(W{:}R)=\Omega(\Delta_N)$ or dissipates $\Omega(\Delta_N)\,k_BT\ln 2$ --- is \emph{not
stateable} in \lean{AITFrame}: $Q_{\rm diss}$ is a physical quantity and the frame has no bath, no
temperature, and no notion of a physical realization of $W$ or $R$. The same holds for the escape
dichotomy, which quantifies over ``any physical realization,'' and for the saturation claim, which
quantifies over sinks of finite capacity. We record the absence as a specification of the work
remaining --- it needs stochastic thermodynamics over a defined coupling --- and not as a result.
The known theorem in that shape is Shannon-form
($\langle W_{\rm ext}\rangle\le-\Delta F+k_BT\,I(W;R)$); whether it admits a single-sequence
algorithmic strengthening with $\Ik$ in place of $I$ is open.

\noindent\textbf{Satisfiability witnesses:} \lean{toy\_symmetry} (symmetry of information holds for
\lean{Toy}), \lean{toyMachine\_coding} (coding theorem holds for \lean{ToyMachine}),
\lean{toy\_self\_model\_fires} (the self-model corollary fires with a genuine gap
$\Delta_{\rm self}=3$). \textbf{Guards:} \lean{\#check\_failure (peers A S)} (part-whole), and
\lean{ystar\_form\_holds} vs \lean{rawy\_form\_fails} (symmetry must use $y^\ast$, not $y$).

\section{Roadmap}
\label{sec:roadmap}
% ══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Priorities are ordered to maximize KT-correctness assurance per unit effort, exploiting the fact
that the AIT/Bayes layer (\S\ref{sec:axioms}) may be assumed.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.4em,style=nextline]
\item[\textcolor{ForestGreen}{Phase~1 --- close the ART chain. \textbf{DONE.}}] The wrapper bound
Eq.~(6) is now a \emph{derived} theorem (\lean{wrapper\_bound}) from the coding theorem (\lean{CodingLB});
ART \textbf{Theorem~1} (\lean{theorem1\_posterior\_tilt}) and \textbf{Theorem~3}
(\lean{theorem3\_onoff\_evidence}, log-free form) are proved; \lean{probabilistic\_regulator\_theorem}
now consumes \lean{wrapper\_bound} and rests on the coding theorem $+$ Lemma~2. All four
\lean{\#print axioms} $=$ Lean core only.
\item[\textcolor{ForestGreen}{Phase~2 --- persistence conservation (WP0162 Prop.~2). \textbf{DONE.}}]
Ledger $K(O_W)=\Ik(O_W{:}R)+K(O_W\mid R^\ast)$ (\lean{persistence\_conservation}, from symmetry of
information) and the trade-off ``max $\Ik$ $=$ min conditional residual'' (\lean{conservation\_tradeoff}).
\item[\textcolor{ForestGreen}{Phase~3 --- regulator selection (WP0162 Prop.~1). \textbf{DONE.}}]
$\argmin_{R}K(R\mid W)=\argmax_R[M(W{:}R)-K(R)]$ proved (\lean{regulator\_selection}) from the
chain rule; the ART sharp form's $2^{-K(R\mid W)}$ reading is
\lean{probabilistic\_regulator\_theorem\_conditional}.
\item[\textcolor{ForestGreen}{Phase~4 --- self-model incompleteness. \textbf{DONE.}}]
The three independent obstructions: the quine floor (\lean{quine\_floor}, $|Q_A|\ge K(A)$),
the self-prediction dichotomy (\lean{self\_prediction\_dichotomy}, an axiom-free diagonalization),
and the Chaitin ceiling on minimality (\lean{chaitin\_blocks\_minimality}).
\item[\textcolor{ForestGreen}{Phase~5 --- coarse-graining uncomputability (WP0193). \textbf{DONE.}}]
Theorem~B and Corollary~B (regulatory coarse-graining uncomputable) proved by an abstract
reduction to Vereshchagin--Vitányi (\lean{theoremB}, \lean{corollaryB}).

\smallskip\noindent\textbf{All five roadmap phases are complete.} The WP0162 / WP0192 / WP0193
KT corollaries and the full probabilistic ART layer are machine-checked, \lean{sorry}-free,
resting only on Lean core plus the named AIT axiom layer of \S\ref{sec:axioms}.
\item[\textcolor{gray}{Later --- geometry track.}] The Lie-group/Noether layer of the world-models
paper is a distinct development (differential geometry in Mathlib), deferred until the AIT/KT core
is complete.
\end{description}
